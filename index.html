Progression Tips

The key to building muscle with bodyweight is progressive overload — add reps, slow down tempo (4 sec down on squats, 3 sec pause on bridges), or reduce rest every 1–2 weeks. When 15 reps feels easy, level up: squats → pause squats → single-leg assisted; glute bridges → single-leg → hip thrusts elevated; knee push-ups → full → decline.
